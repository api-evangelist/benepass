# Benepass

> Benepass is a financial-technology platform for flexible, compliant employee
> benefits — one platform and one card covering pre-tax accounts (HSA, FSA, HRA),
> Lifestyle Spending Accounts (LSAs), and programs for wellness, professional
> development, family care, food, and commuter benefits, built for global teams.
> Benepass connects to employer systems through HRIS/payroll integrations (API,
> SFTP, and Workday RaaS); it does not currently publish a public developer API.
